# Rev69 — Unified Industrial Training Facility

Rev69 connects the Rev68 forensic laboratory to topology-aware instruments, transient insulation testing, reference-standard transmitter service, complete DP practical state, adaptive transient stepping, executable PLC instruction state, protocol request/response records, educational CAN arbitration/error-frame behavior, tiered forensic buffers, and cross-surface identity synchronization.

## Engineering rules
- UI surfaces consume published state; they do not calculate electrical/process truth.
- Instrument insertion changes circuit topology or loading where appropriate.
- Calibration records reference-standard uncertainty separately from device error.
- DP practicals require restoration/as-left state, not merely a zero observation.
- CAN models are educational physical/protocol models, not an OEM implementation.
- Safety thresholds and procedures remain training constructs unless separately verified against applicable current requirements/site procedures.
