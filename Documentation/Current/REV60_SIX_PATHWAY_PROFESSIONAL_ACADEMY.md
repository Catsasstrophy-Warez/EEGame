# Rev60 Six-Pathway Professional Academy

Rev60 deepens six career pathways: Apprentice Electrician, Journeyman Electrician, Master Electrician, I&E Technician, Controls Technician, and Commissioning Specialist.

## Design rules
- One physical truth: training uses the same simulation, instruments, equipment identity, Golden Thread, faults, and evidence as the game.
- Career perspective changes the task, not the underlying equipment truth.
- Safety is a gate, not bonus XP.
- Assessment emphasizes evidence, measurement planning, repair verification, documentation, and oral defense.
- Code/navigation exercises are original and do not reproduce copyrighted NEC or commercial training question banks.
- Mike Holt is used as a secondary curriculum-structure reference; OSHA/current authoritative sources govern regulatory claims.

## Scale
- 6 professional pathways.
- 20 major topic families per pathway (120 total).
- 8 progression bands from Foundation through Capstone.
- 72 practical stations, each supporting 64 deterministic fault/configuration variants (4,608 variant slots before combinatorial equipment/document/instrument changes).
- 6 multi-hour professional capstones with 12 phases each.
- Cross-career scenarios reuse the same equipment from six professional perspectives.

## Training loop
Learn -> calculate -> trace drawings -> build/inspect -> measure -> commission -> diagnose -> correct -> prove -> document -> defend -> transfer.
