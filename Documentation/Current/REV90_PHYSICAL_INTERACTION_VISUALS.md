# Rev90 Physical Interaction & Production Visuals

Rev90 turns the Rev89 spatial equipment layer into a more tactile diagnostic surface while preserving the shared Rev88 physical measurement truth.

Added: animated MCC door state; physical conductor harness; selectable meter probe placement; animated scope waveform; breaker/fuse/contactor cutaways; calculated thermal wear/discoloration surface; direct Field-page reachability; accessibility IDs.

All displayed electrical quantities remain derived from `EEFacilityMeasurementFrame88`. Rev90 does not create a parallel telemetry model.

Next production ceiling: RealityKit geometry and cabinet materials, terminal-to-terminal 3D conductor routing, mechanically animated disconnect/contact assemblies, buffered transient samples rather than display-only sine visualization, interactive lead drag/drop, process machinery animation, facility environmental lighting/audio, and authored equipment aging variants.
