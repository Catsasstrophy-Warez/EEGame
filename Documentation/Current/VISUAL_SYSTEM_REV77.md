# Rev77 Spatial Industrial Twin

Rev77 deepens the Rev76 visual correction from industrial-themed panels into spatial, inspectable equipment.

Implemented:
- station route/map from electrical room to MCC, field JB and process skid;
- cabinet states: closed, door open, dead-front open and bucket withdrawn;
- VFD keypad/readout;
- motor starter/fuse representation;
- PLC rack with live module LEDs;
- wire duct;
- DIN rail terminal strip with TB1:12 emphasis;
- wire-numbered conductor routes;
- withdrawable MCC bucket visualization;
- DMM lead/probe overlay;
- persistent selected identity through cabinet, Electrical Vision and Golden Thread.

Visual rule: graphics must expose simulation identity and interaction. Decorative realism alone is not sufficient.

Next production visual targets:
1. drag-and-drop probe placement with terminal hit testing;
2. cabinet zoom/pan and cutaway transitions;
3. schematic canvas with the same conductor IDs;
4. thermal camera shader/overlay;
5. current-flow particles and voltage potential map;
6. VFD and PLC faceplates backed by real runtime state;
7. 3D RealityKit equipment twins after 2D interaction contracts are stable.
