# Rev82 Immersive Industrial Twin

Rev82 deepens the RealityKit layer into an authored industrial training scene.

Implemented:
- MCC shell, front door, dead front and withdrawable starter bucket;
- disconnect, three fuse bodies, contactor, overload, PLC rack/modules, DIN rail, 16-position terminal strip and wire duct;
- routed W-1207 segments to JB-14, SOL-101, XV-101 and PIT-101;
- cabinet access states;
- Normal, Electrical, Thermal and Golden Thread 3D visual modes;
- telemetry-driven material updates;
- localized failure heat signatures derived from scenario state;
- spatial tap selection continues to use the same facility identity;
- tests for Golden Thread continuity and fault-dependent thermal behavior.

RealityKit remains a presentation of ScenarioEngine truth. No independent 3D physics or diagnostic answers are introduced.
