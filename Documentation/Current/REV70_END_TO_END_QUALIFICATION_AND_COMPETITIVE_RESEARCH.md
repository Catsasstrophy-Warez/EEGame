# Rev70 — End-to-End Qualification Jobs + Competitive Research

Rev70 turns the Rev69 unified facility into complete I&E and Controls qualification jobs. Jobs progress from complaint and documents through safety, as-found condition, physical inspection, measurements, competing hypotheses, discriminating tests, first divergence, root cause, repair, calibration/configuration, recommissioning, functional proof, as-left evidence, documentation and oral defense.

## Flagship jobs
- IE-70-001: intermittent discharge-pressure divergence. Hidden truth is a vibration-sensitive high-resistance termination; the same complaint can later be generated from impulse restriction, transmitter configuration, loop power, AI, scaling, network, HMI or historian faults.
- CTRL-70-001: first-divergence controls investigation. Field signal is healthy but engineering scaling is incorrect after a configuration change.

## Competitive research assimilation
The detailed research pass covers Automation Studio, Factory I/O, PLCLogix, LogixPro/LadderLab, PLC Fiddle, CODESYS, VirtualPlant, CircuitVerse, Proteus VSM, Tinkercad Circuits, Turing Complete, SHENZHEN I/O, PhET Circuit Construction Kit, and IndustrialSim. The project should adapt patterns, not proprietary UI/code/content.

Highest-value patterns to retain:
1. Multi-technology co-simulation and instruments (Automation Studio).
2. Fast 3D scene authoring, external PLC connectivity, instructor fault injection (Factory I/O).
3. Familiar ladder editor + I/O chassis + 3D process context (PLCLogix).
4. Small focused ladder exercises and process simulations (LogixPro/LadderLab).
5. Zero-friction browser ladder experimentation (PLC Fiddle).
6. Professional IEC 61131-3 engineering, online debugging, visualization, reusable libraries (CODESYS).
7. Gamified virtual engineering labs and procedural practice (VirtualPlant category).
8. Hierarchical subcircuits, buses, timing diagrams, testbenches, classroom sharing (CircuitVerse).
9. Firmware + mixed-mode circuit co-simulation with source-level debugging (Proteus VSM).
10. Extremely low-friction breadboard/Arduino construction (Tinkercad Circuits).
11. Primitive-to-system mastery progression (Turing Complete).
12. Manuals/datasheets as gameplay and constraint-solving (SHENZHEN I/O).
13. Lifelike/schematic dual representation, visible current and direct meters (PhET CCK).
14. Field-oriented motor-control/PLC practice, guided labs, auto-checking, component library (IndustrialSim).

Electric Engineer differentiator: one physical truth spanning electrical physics, process, instruments, PLC execution, network evidence, degradation, maintenance history, forensics, commissioning and professional qualification.
