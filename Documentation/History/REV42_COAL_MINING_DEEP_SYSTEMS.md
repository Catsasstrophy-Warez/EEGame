# Rev42 Coal & Mining Deep Systems

Rev42 deepens only the coal/mining world. Natural-gas process state remains architecturally separate.

Implemented coal-specific foundations:
- independent deep systems for all three fictional mines
- longwall shearer/AFC/stage-loader/crusher state
- continuous-mining/development section
- underground power center and ground-monitor/ground-check states
- underground belt permissives, pull cords, alignment, zero-speed, belt-tear, bearing heat and CO consequence
- ventilation network branches/regulators
- multi-stage mine-water pumping
- mass-weighted raw-coal quality blending
- individual preparation machine families
- magnetite medium circuit
- flotation bank
- thickener bed/torque/clarity dynamics
- 212-car batch loadout foundation
- coal-specific evidence/work-order generation
- persistence

Research reference, not exact site replication: public SEC technical reports for the Pennsylvania Mining Complex and other Appalachian prep plants, plus MSHA longwall/electrical materials. Numerical gameplay thresholds are educational model parameters unless explicitly sourced and versioned.
