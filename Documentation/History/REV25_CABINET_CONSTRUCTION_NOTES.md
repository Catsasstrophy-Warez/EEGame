# Rev25 Cabinet Construction Truth

Rev25 promotes cabinet construction details into executable simulation state: termination hardware/condition, terminal-strip end stops and jumpers, fused/disconnect terminals, cable-core fanout, shield clamps, door bonding/harness fatigue, derived duct occupancy, and enclosure thermal/humidity control.

These models are educational engineering abstractions. They are not NEC/NFPA/UL compliance determinations and do not replace manufacturer installation instructions or qualified-person procedures.

Validation: 290/290 Swift Testing tests passed with Swift 6.2.1 on Linux. Apple/Xcode/iOS/RealityKit validation remains separate.
