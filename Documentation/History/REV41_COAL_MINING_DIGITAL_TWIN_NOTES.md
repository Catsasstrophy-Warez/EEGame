# Rev41 — Coal & Mining Digital Twin

Rev41 adds a second industrial world while preserving a hard domain boundary from the natural-gas compressor-station simulation. Coal/mining models never contain or reference gas-process runtime state.

Research reference only: public descriptions of the Pennsylvania Mining Complex (Bailey, Enlow Fork, Harvey and Central CPP) informed scale and system coverage. The game uses fictional mine names and does not claim to reproduce proprietary plant layouts, control logic, operating procedures, or safety limits.

Implemented foundations: three independent underground mine feeds, overland/slope conveyors, nine mine-segregated ROM silos, two-module 8,200-TPH-class preparation architecture, heavy-media/flotation-oriented prep abstraction, six clean-coal silos, water/refuse circuit, independent mine ventilation and pumping, rail batch-loadout model, electrical loading, synchronized engineering identities, persistence, and a SwiftUI coal-world dashboard.

Future depth: longwall/shearer/AFC/shields, continuous mining, section power, ground monitoring, belt fire/CO monitoring, detailed transfer towers and takeups, screens/crushers/baths/cyclones/spirals/flotation/magnetite, centrifuges, thickeners, coarse/fine refuse, detailed water balance, train positioning/weighing/sampling, mine communications, ventilation network solver, power-flow/protection, condition monitoring, and emergent coal-specific work orders.
