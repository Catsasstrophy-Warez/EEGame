# Rev57 Deep Comprehensive Training System

Rev57 expands training from a lesson list into a competency graph tied to simulation truth.

## Research discipline
Public topic descriptions and training structures from Mike Holt are used as curriculum inspiration/reference only. No proprietary Mike Holt textbook, workbook, illustration, question bank, or paid course content is copied. NEC requirements must be verified against the edition adopted by the applicable jurisdiction and the AHJ. OSHA regulatory references are tracked separately from consensus standards and third-party training sources.

Reference families include Mike Holt Understanding Electrical Theory, Understanding the NEC, Bonding and Grounding, and Fundamental NEC Calculations; OSHA 29 CFR 1910.332 electrical training; OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 hazardous-energy control; NFPA 70; and NFPA 70E.

## Training architecture
- 36 training domains
- six difficulty bands: Foundation, Apprentice, Journeyman, Advanced, Master, Forensic
- 11 experiential stages inherited from Rev56
- skill prerequisites instead of only lesson prerequisites
- adaptive mastery per skill
- spaced reinforcement
- cross-domain transfer challenges
- evidence-based scoring
- safety gates
- deterministic instructor scenarios
- randomized fault families
- instrument constraints
- simulation-provenance evidence requirements

## Curriculum families
Electrical theory; DC; AC; calculations; code navigation; wiring methods; grounding/bonding; overcurrent protection; services/feeders/branch circuits; transformers; generators; motors; motor controls; industrial power; hazardous locations; electrical safety; LOTO; test instruments; electronics; instrumentation; calibration; PLC; HMI/SCADA; drives; industrial networks; embedded; CAN; process control; commissioning; maintenance/reliability; troubleshooting; forensic diagnostics; natural-gas I&E; coal-mining I&E; engineering analysis; documentation; leadership/instruction.

## Learning loop
Observe -> Explain -> Guided Build -> Independent Build -> Measure -> Commission -> Faulted Diagnose -> Repair -> Prove -> Teach Back -> Mastery Challenge.

A learner is not awarded mastery merely for selecting the correct answer. Attempts can be evaluated on evidence quality, measurement efficiency, root-cause correctness, repair verification, explanation quality, transfer performance, hint dependence, and safety behavior. Unsafe actions cap the attempt score.

## Safety
This is an educational simulation, not a substitute for employer procedures, qualified-person training, site-specific safe-work practices, adopted electrical codes, OSHA compliance, or NFPA 70E programs.
