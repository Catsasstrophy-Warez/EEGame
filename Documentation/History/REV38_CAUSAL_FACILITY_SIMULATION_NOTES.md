# Rev38 — Causal Facility Simulation

Rev38 expands the living station into an integrated process/electrical/automation/maintenance digital twin. It adds authored process equipment identities, a compressor unit, anti-surge controller, power distribution thermal state, instrument-air utility, remote I/O truth, ESD causes, maintenance/PdM scheduling, alarm rationalization primitives, multi-fault incidents, and procedural diagnostic cases generated from plant state.

## Design rule
One causal facility truth. Process, electrical, controls, historian, alarms, work orders, maintenance, and training cases are observations or consequences of the same evolving state.

## Research-informed behavior
Public industrial references support fast recycle/anti-surge action, compressor condition monitoring, dynamic operator training, PLC simulation/data monitoring, and diagnostic instrumentation. Exact OEM algorithms, proprietary thresholds, and site procedures are intentionally not replicated. Model numbers and thresholds in the game remain educational scenario data unless separately verified.

## Rev39 candidates
Multi-unit suction/discharge header interactions; separator carryover; cooler fouling; reciprocating-compressor cylinder physics; full anti-surge map visualization; ESD cause/effect matrix; alarm shelving/suppression; historian compression/deadband; network topology and packet loss; calibration drift; maintenance backlog; spare-parts procurement; shift scheduling; weather ingress; procedural generation with causal chains; full facility P&ID/single-line/loop-sheet synchronization.
