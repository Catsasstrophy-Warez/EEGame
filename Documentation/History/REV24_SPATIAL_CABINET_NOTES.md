# Rev24 Spatial Cabinet Authoring

Rev24 promotes panel archetypes into spatially authored cabinets. It adds DIN rails, wire duct, door-mounted devices, terminals, cable glands, routed wires, continuity traversal, and documentation projection from shared identities.

Research-informed patterns used as design inputs:
- MCCs use dedicated horizontal and vertical wireways and modular functional units.
- Process marshalling emphasizes dense DIN-rail terminal/marshalling systems with accessible test points and clear identification.
- Burner-management installations require distinct ignition/flame-sensing/grounding paths and careful field wiring.

The geometry/audit rules in the simulation are educational heuristics. They are not declarations of NEC, UL, NFPA, hazardous-location, manufacturer, or site compliance.

## Canonical cabinet identities
A terminal, wire number, cable gland, DIN rail, duct, door device, and panel component can now retain one stable identity across the physical cabinet and generated documentation.
