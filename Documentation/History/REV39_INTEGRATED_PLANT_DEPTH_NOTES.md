# Rev39 — Integrated Plant Depth

Rev39 deepens Rev38 into a multi-domain causal plant model. Added: reciprocating compressor cylinder/valve condition, separator liquid/carryover dynamics, cooler fouling, pneumatic recycle-valve response, delayed motor protection, 24 VDC control power/battery behavior, PLC rack/module health, industrial network link quality, historian deadband/compression policy, alarm first-out/shelving/suppression foundation, calibration drift, maintenance backlog, inspection routes, spare-part reorder state, and ESD cause/effect actions.

Design rule remains: one physical truth feeds process, PLC, HMI, historian, alarms, instruments and visualizations. Numerical thresholds in these educational models are scenario parameters, not OEM, regulatory, or site procedures.
