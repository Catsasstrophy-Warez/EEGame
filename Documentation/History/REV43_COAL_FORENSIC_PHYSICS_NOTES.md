# Rev43 Coal Mine & Preparation Plant Forensic Physics

Coal/mining only. Natural-gas process state is deliberately not added or modified.

Adds individual longwall shields and hydraulics, shearer/AFC forensic states, atmospheric ventilation branches, spatial belt/idler heating and CO source behavior, mass-conserving coal/water streams, heavy-media separation, flotation cells, thickener dynamics, delayed coal-lab samples, and phase-driven train loadout.

All numerical process constants are educational simulation values unless explicitly documented otherwise. They are not mine operating limits, safety criteria, regulatory thresholds, or OEM settings.
