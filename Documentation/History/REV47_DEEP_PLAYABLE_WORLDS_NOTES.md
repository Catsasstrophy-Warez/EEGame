# Rev47 — Deep Playable Worlds

Rev47 expands production integration rather than merging industrial process truth.

## Electronics Bench
- Ten experiment families from DC fundamentals through CAN.
- Physical node identities, movable red/black instrument leads, and measurements generated from bench state.
- Bench evidence carries `simulation-truth` provenance.

## Training & Instruments
- Eleven schools: fundamentals, motor controls, industrial power, instrumentation, PLC/automation, drives, networks, process controls, advanced diagnostics, coal I&E, and natural-gas I&E.
- Field-lab progression and XP foundation tied to instrument families.

## Golden Thread 2.0
- Rev46 registries plus spatial assets in both industrial worlds.
- Search can resolve asset, tag, drawing, and physical-location references.
- Gas and coal identities retain explicit world ownership.

## Interactive instruments and evidence
- Instruments can be placed against live forensic channels for selected spatial assets.
- Captures enter the Evidence Board with world, timestamp, identity, quantity, value, unit and provenance.
- Competing hypotheses normalize as supporting/contradicting evidence is ingested.

## Spatial worlds
- Natural gas: inlet/separator, MCC/VFD, compressor building, recycle, discharge instrumentation, dehydration.
- Coal/mining: longwall, overland conveyor, raw storage, CPP, water/refuse, train loadout.
- Advancing one world does not advance the other.
