# Rev37 — Living Compressor Station

Rev37 promotes the persistent shift into a continuously evolving facility. The station now has spatial zones and travel time, autonomous process/PID behavior, environmental state, alarms and radio/operator calls, isolation/permit truth, tool-truck inventory, synchronized historian/network captures, shift handoff records, and work orders generated from machine condition.

## Design rule
The plant does not wait for the player. Travel, diagnosis and document review consume simulation time. Equipment and process states continue evolving while the player is elsewhere.

## Safety boundary
Isolation/permit mechanics are educational simulation state, not a substitute for site-specific LOTO, electrical safe-work practices, procedures, training, or applicable regulations.
