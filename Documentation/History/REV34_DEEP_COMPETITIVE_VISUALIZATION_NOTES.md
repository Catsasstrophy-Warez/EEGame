# Rev34 — Deep Competitive Visualization & Training Systems

Rev34 turns the Rev33 competitive feature map into executable domain models and production SwiftUI visualization surfaces.

## New executable systems
- Visualization registry: maps canonical identities to visual surfaces, primitives, source paths, units and interaction state.
- Engineering Analysis Lab: real-time, DC operating point, transient, AC, sweeps, tolerance, thermal, harmonics, power quality, timing and network trace modes.
- Trace to Reality: reverse navigation from PLC tag through I/O, signal conditioning, terminals, cable, transmitter and process.
- Work-order journey: briefing through closeout with explicit proof-of-repair phases.
- Instructor Studio: tool/document restrictions, guidance level, hidden-fault mode, demonstrations and simple tag overrides.
- CAN Bench: physical bus fault taxonomy and termination consequence model.
- Deep competitive catalog: 16 feature families with adaptation, visualization and shared-truth hook.

## New SwiftUI visualizations
`Rev34DeepVisualLabView` includes synchronized representation switching, Electrical Vision, Golden Thread visualization, Instrument Locker, evidence/hypothesis board, work-order progress, Engineering Analysis Lab, Trace to Reality, CAN waveform/fault bench and competitive assimilation atlas.

## Design boundary
Competitor research is used as product-pattern inspiration. No proprietary UI, art, source code, authored scenarios, trademarked visual identity, or vendor firmware behavior is copied. Where actual equipment behavior is represented, public/OEM sources remain authoritative and educational abstractions are labeled accordingly.
