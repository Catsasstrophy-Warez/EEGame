# Rev44 — Coal Systems Forensic Depth

Rev44 deepens only the coal/mining world. Natural-gas process models remain separate and unchanged.

Implemented coal-only systems:
- longwall hydraulic manifold/accumulator and solenoid foundation
- shearer drum/bit/bearing/gearbox health
- AFC chain pretension, elongation, sprocket and gearbox dynamics
- continuous-miner/roof-bolter/haulage forensic state
- nonlinear mine-fan curve foundation and distributed atmospheric sensors
- mine pump curve/head/cavitation foundation
- belt take-up tension/carriage dynamics
- corrected/dilute heavy-medium loop, density bias, magnetic recovery
- drain/rinse screen recovery and blinding
- centrifuge moisture/vibration/bearing thermal behavior
- dust collector differential pressure/loading/hopper state
- silo ratholing/bridging/outflow behavior
- delayed coal sampler cadence
- 212-car instrumented train-loadout foundation
- synchronized coal incident recorder for replay

Safety note: numerical thresholds and simplified equations in these educational models are not mine operating limits, OEM settings, regulatory criteria, or field procedures. Actual mine safety decisions require current approved mine plans, MSHA requirements, OEM documentation, and site procedures.

Validation: 546/546 Swift tests across 12 suites on Swift 6.2.1/Linux.
