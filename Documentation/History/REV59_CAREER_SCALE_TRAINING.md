# Rev59 — Career-Scale Training & Assessment

Rev59 turns the Rev58 academy into a career-scale competency and assessment system. It adds eight pathways (Apprentice, Journeyman, Master Electrician, I&E Technician, Controls Technician, Commissioning Specialist, Forensic Engineer, Instructor), 96 original code-navigation exercises, 72 oral/teach-back defenses, deterministic randomized practical variants, calculation→prediction→simulation→measurement labs, instructor dashboards, and scenario authoring.

## Source discipline
Mike Holt Enterprises is used only as a curriculum-shape and public-topic reference. No proprietary textbook passages, illustrations, workbook questions, simulated exams, or answer banks are reproduced. The 2026 public catalog confirms distinct training families for electrical theory, NEC Volumes 1/2, bonding and grounding, fundamental calculations, and exam preparation. OSHA 1910.332, 1910.333, and 1910.147 are authority references for electrical training/work-practice and hazardous-energy concepts. Actual field work remains governed by current law, adopted codes, employer procedures, equipment instructions, and qualified-person requirements.

## Assessment philosophy
Knowledge is necessary but insufficient. High-level completion requires physical evidence, drawing interpretation, appropriate instrument use, safe work planning, root-cause reasoning, repair verification, documentation, transfer to unfamiliar equipment, and oral defense. Randomized physical faults prevent answer memorization. Difficulty changes assistance and information exposure, never the underlying physical truth.
