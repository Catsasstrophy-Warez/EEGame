# Rev40 — Forensic Plant Physics

Rev40 deepens Rev39 into a cross-domain forensic model rather than a collection of independent feature demos.

Implemented foundations:
- shared suction/discharge headers and compressor load sharing
- reciprocating compressor throw/cylinder P-V diagram generation and gas rod-load estimate
- order-based vibration spectrum model with valve-impact signatures
- induction-motor equivalent-circuit/slip/current/copper-loss model
- VFD rectifier/precharge/DC-bus/inverter state with ripple and capacitor-health hooks
- time-current protection curves and selectivity comparison
- battery-backed 24 VDC UPS/charger behavior
- per-channel analog-input electronics/fault modes and ADC quantization
- managed Ethernet switch ports and redundant-uplink failover foundation
- historian interpolation/reconstruction
- valve command/travel signature generation with stiction and air-pressure authority
- calibration as-found/as-left records
- synchronized identity bindings across P&ID, one-line, elementary, loop, ladder, HMI and 3D surfaces

Research discipline: numerical values in these educational models are scenario data, not universal OEM settings or field procedures. Real equipment requires its current manufacturer documentation, site engineering standards, protection studies, and applicable safety procedures.
