# Rev56 — Numerical Validation + Mastery Training

Rev56 deepens two coupled pillars: numerical credibility and evidence-based technical training.

## Numerical validation
- Independent analytical waveform harness for RC and RL canonical circuits.
- Backward Euler and trapezoidal integration run side-by-side against closed-form references.
- Per-run maximum and RMS error metrics with explicit tolerances.
- KCL, KVL, and power-conservation audit foundation.
- Designed to expand into cross-solver netlist comparison, Newton damping/source stepping/gmin stepping, semiconductor curve validation, and large sparse-network torture cases.

## Training expansion
The curriculum now defines 16 schools and 80 authored lesson templates. Schools include fundamentals, electronics, motor controls, industrial power, instrumentation, PLC/automation, drives, networks, process controls, advanced diagnostics, natural-gas I&E, coal-mining I&E, embedded systems, engineering analysis, safety/commissioning, and forensic engineering.

The mastery ladder expands to 11 stages: Observe, Explain, Guided Build, Independent Build, Measure, Commission, Faulted Diagnose, Repair, Prove, Teach Back, Mastery Challenge.

Fourteen competency dimensions are tracked independently: theory, schematic reading, physical construction, instrument use, measurement planning, safety/isolation, commissioning, diagnosis, root cause, repair verification, documentation, automation, process reasoning, and numerical reasoning.

Assessment is evidence-based. Completion depends on evidence, correct root cause, verified repair, hint usage, and safe execution. Unsafe actions cap the assessment below completion threshold. Mastery uses weighted updates across repeated attempts rather than a single binary pass.

## Training design rule
Difficulty changes assistance and truth exposure, never underlying physics. Lessons operate on the same simulation, instruments, evidence, Golden Thread, industrial assets, and forensic timeline used by the rest of the game.
